Getting Here & Venue Details

Travel & Venues

Jamshedpur is easily accessible by rail and air, with celebrations hosted across Sonari West and the serene fairways of Beldhi Golf Course.

Reaching Jamshedpur

By Rail — Tatanagar (TATA)

~6 km • 15–20 min drive

Tatanagar Junction is well connected via Vande Bharat, Shatabdi, Rajdhani, and express trains from Kolkata (Howrah), New Delhi, Mumbai, and Bhubaneswar. Cabs and autos are readily available outside the station.

By Air — Ranchi / Kolkata

IXR (~2.5–3 hrs) • CCU + Rail

Birsa Munda Airport, Ranchi (IXR) is ~125 km away via NH-33 by cab. Alternatively, fly to Kolkata (CCU) and board a 3.5–4 hr express train to Tatanagar. Sonari Airport (IXW) serves select regional routes (~5 km).
